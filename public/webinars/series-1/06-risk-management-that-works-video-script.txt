PCMO WEBINAR VIDEO PRODUCTION SCRIPT
Series 1 - Webinar 06

TITLE: Risk Management That Actually Works
AUDIENCE: All project and contract professionals
OBJECTIVE: Make risk management a decision process rather than a reporting ritual.

OPENING
Welcome to PCMO Series One: Project Management Fundamentals. In this webinar, we focus on risk management that supports real decisions.

SCENE 1 - RISK REGISTERS
Record clear cause-event-effect statements, categories, owners, probability, impact, proximity, controls, responses, actions, dates, residual exposure, and links to cost and schedule.

SCENE 2 - RISK OWNERSHIP
The risk owner must have appropriate accountability and influence. Action owners complete specific responses, while governance resolves exposure beyond delegated authority.

SCENE 3 - RISK RESPONSES
Avoid, reduce, transfer, share, accept, or exploit uncertainty through specific actions. Evaluate cost, timing, secondary risk, contractual allocation, and the evidence that a response is working.

SCENE 4 - PRACTICAL APPLICATION
Use risk information in estimates, schedules, procurement, contracts, design, contingency, forecasts, change decisions, assurance, and executive escalation - not only in monthly reports.

CLOSING CALL TO ACTION
Select the highest current exposure and confirm its owner, response, due date, decision threshold, and evidence of effectiveness.
