PCMO WEBINAR VIDEO PRODUCTION SCRIPT
Series 1 - Webinar 04

TITLE: Project Scheduling Masterclass
AUDIENCE: Project controls and delivery professionals
OBJECTIVE: Use schedule logic to support credible commitments, forecasts, and recovery decisions.

OPENING
Welcome to PCMO Series One: Project Management Fundamentals. In this webinar, we examine the foundations of reliable project scheduling.

SCENE 1 - CRITICAL PATH METHOD
Connect activities through realistic logic, durations, calendars, constraints, resources, and milestones so the schedule explains the sequence that controls completion.

SCENE 2 - FLOAT
Treat float as decision information, not free time. Understand total and free float, ownership questions, consumption, near-critical paths, and the effect of imposed constraints.

SCENE 3 - BASELINES
An approved baseline records the authorised delivery plan. It must be realistic, traceable, integrated with scope and cost, and changed only through controlled authority.

SCENE 4 - RECOVERY SCHEDULES
Recovery starts with verified progress and remaining scope. Test productivity, logic, resources, interfaces, risks, contractual constraints, and alternative scenarios before committing to a date.

CLOSING CALL TO ACTION
Challenge your next schedule review to explain the controlling path, major assumptions, float trend, risk exposure, and decision required.
